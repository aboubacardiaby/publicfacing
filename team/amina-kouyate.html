Amina Kouyaté

Mobile Developer

Specialises in React Native and Flutter, creating smooth cross-platform apps with native-quality feel and performance.

About

Amina builds the mobile apps that Talence Informatixs ships to the App Store and Google Play. She works across both React Native and Flutter, and rather than picking a favorite, she matches the framework to the project — team background, UI complexity, and platform-specific integration needs all factor into the call before a single screen gets built.

She cares a lot about the details that make an app feel "native" even when it's cross-platform: gesture handling that matches platform conventions, animations that don't drop frames on older devices, and offline behavior that doesn't leave users staring at a spinner. Getting an app through App Store and Play Store review without a rejection cycle is practically a specialty of hers at this point.

About

Sarah leads frontend engineering at Talence Informatixs, where she's spent the last several years turning design files into interfaces that hold up under real-world use — slow networks, screen readers, and the inevitable edge case nobody thought to design for. She's especially drawn to design systems: the kind of foundational work that lets a whole team ship consistent UI faster without reinventing components every sprint.

Accessibility isn't an afterthought in her process — it's baked into component libraries from the start, which means client projects launch WCAG-compliant instead of retrofitted after an audit finds problems. She also keeps a close eye on performance budgets, treating page weight and load time as design constraints rather than an engineering afterthought.

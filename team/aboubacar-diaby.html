About

Aboubacar founded Talence Informatixs to bridge the gap between ambitious business goals and the technology needed to reach them. He splits his time between setting the company's technical direction and staying directly involved in client engagements — particularly the complex, high-stakes system integration and modernization projects where a wrong architectural call can cost a business years.

Before founding Talence Informatixs, he spent years working across enterprise integration and infrastructure roles, which shaped his conviction that the biggest risk in technology projects usually isn't the technology itself — it's rushing a big, irreversible decision when a smaller, reversible one would do. That philosophy now runs through how the whole team scopes and delivers work.
